About NutriTrack AI

NutriTrack AI is a college-project nutrition analysis prototype designed to help users understand the nutrients present in foods and track their dietary intake over 14 days.

How It Works

Search for a food or upload a food image, select the quantity, view its nutrition information, and add it to your daily tracker.


The prototype uses a local food database. A real AI vision API and backend database can be connected later.

Medical Disclaimer

This app provides general nutritional and educational information. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Nutrients such as iron, folate, vitamin B12, vitamin C, vitamin B6 and protein contribute to normal blood and red blood cell functions. The app does not predict hemoglobin levels or guarantee a specific increase in hemoglobin.
