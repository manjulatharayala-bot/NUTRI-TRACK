ℹ️ About NutriTrack AI

NutriTrack AI is a college-project prototype designed to help users understand the nutritional content of the foods they eat.


🩸 Blood Support

Iron, folate, vitamin B12, vitamin B6, vitamin C and protein contribute to normal blood and red blood cell functions.


This app provides general nutritional and educational information. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Nutrition values are approximate prototype values and can vary depending on food variety, preparation and serving size.
